2-Year Limited Warranty

Red Rose Custom Closets warrants that all custom closet systems we manufacture and install are free from defects in materials and workmanship for a period of two (2) years from the date of installation.

What This Warranty Covers

  • Material defects
  • Workmanship defects
  • Hardware functionality (hinges, drawer slides, handles)

What This Warranty Does Not Cover

  • Normal wear and tear
  • Misuse or overloading
  • Environmental factors (humidity, water, sunlight, temperature)
  • Improper care or maintenance
  • Unauthorized modifications or repairs
  • Structural or building-related issues
  • Damage from accidents or external forces

Third-Party Accessories

The following are not covered under this warranty:

  • Valet rods
  • Pull-out laundry baskets / hampers
  • Specialty inserts or organizers
  • Any outsourced or branded accessory components

These items may carry their own manufacturer warranty.

Limitations

  • Non-transferable
  • Applies only to original purchaser
  • Limited to repair or replacement
  • No liability for indirect or consequential damages

How to Make a Claim

Contact us with your order details and photos of the issue. Our team will review and determine eligibility.
